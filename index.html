Privacy Policy for Football Club Empire

Developed by Hasan Can Guler · Last updated 13 October 2026

Gameplay data

Saved careers and game settings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us. Separately, the advertising and analytics services described below collect information about use of the app.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, ad views and interactions, crash logs, and performance information. This information is used to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and improve its services.

On iOS, access to your device's advertising identifier and tracking across other companies' apps and websites require your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can manage your advertising ID through your device's privacy or advertising settings. The exact steps depend on your device and Android version.

For information about Google's handling of data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players use the app and improve it. Analytics collects information such as app launches, sessions, purchase events, device and operating system information, app-instance identifiers, and approximate location derived from masked IP addresses. Purchase events may include the product purchased and its price, but do not include payment card details.

For more information, see Firebase's Privacy and Security information and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Apple’s App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android, where purchases are available. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase information may be used to provide purchased features and is also recorded by Firebase Analytics as described above.

Data retention and deletion

Local saved careers and settings remain on your device until they are deleted. Removing the app's local data does not automatically delete information already collected by advertising or analytics services.

Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings configured for our Google Analytics property. Google handles advertising data according to its policies.

To ask about access to or deletion of data associated with your use of the app, contact us at the email address below. We may need additional information to identify the relevant app installation, because the game does not require a user account.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact support.footballclubempire@gmail.com.
