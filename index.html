Privacy Policy for Football Club Empire

Developed by Hasan Can Guler · Last updated 13 October 2026

Gameplay data

Saved careers and game settings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us. The game does not require a user account. Separately, the advertising, analytics, and crash-reporting services described below collect information about your device and use of the app.

Club finances, player salaries, fitness, and other simulated information within the game are gameplay data, rather than information about your personal finances or health.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. The advertising service may initialize and request ads before you choose to watch a rewarded video, subject to applicable consent requirements.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, ad views and interactions, crash logs, and performance information. This information is used to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and improve its services. Where permitted and subject to your consent choices, advertising may be personalized.

On iOS, access to your device's advertising identifier and tracking across other companies' apps and websites require your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this permission under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining tracking permission does not necessarily prevent ads from appearing.

On Android, you can manage your advertising ID through your device's privacy or advertising settings. The exact steps depend on your device and Android version.

For information about Google's handling of data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players use the app and improve it. When enabled, Analytics collects information such as app launches, sessions, purchase events, device and operating system information, app-instance identifiers, and approximate location derived from masked IP addresses. Purchase events may include the product purchased and its price, but do not include payment card details.

Analytics collection starts disabled and is enabled according to the consent status provided by Google's consent service.

For more information, see Firebase's Privacy and Security information.

Crash reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix crashes and improve the app's stability. Crashlytics collects crash reports, stack traces, installation identifiers, device and operating system information, and related application state and diagnostic information.

When Analytics is enabled, crash reports may also include activity information, known as breadcrumbs, that helps explain what happened before a crash. Crash reporting operates separately from Analytics consent and may remain active when Analytics collection is disabled.

For more information, see Firebase's Privacy and Security information.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Apple's App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android, where purchases are available. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

The app processes purchase and transaction information to provide purchased features, confirm transactions, and restore eligible purchases. When Analytics is enabled, purchase events are also recorded by Firebase Analytics as described above.

Privacy choices

Where available, you can review or change your advertising consent choices using the privacy options in the game's main menu. The consent options shown depend on your location and applicable requirements.

Your advertising consent choices and Apple's tracking permission are separate controls. You can also manage tracking permission and advertising identifiers through your device settings as described above. Changing a consent choice does not automatically delete data previously collected.

Data sharing

Information described in this policy is processed by the relevant service providers, including Google for advertising, analytics, and crash reporting, and Apple or Google for purchases. Their handling of information is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Local saved careers and settings remain on your device until they are deleted. Removing the app's local data does not automatically delete information already collected by advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Analytics data is retained according to the retention settings configured for our Google Analytics property. Advertising and crash-reporting data are retained according to the relevant service providers' policies and applicable service settings. Purchase records may also be retained by Apple or Google under their policies and legal obligations.

To ask about access to or deletion of data associated with your use of the app, contact us at the email address below. We may need additional information to identify the relevant app installation because the game does not require a user account. Our ability to identify or delete data depends on the information available to us and the controls provided by the relevant service.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact support.footballclubempire@gmail.com.
