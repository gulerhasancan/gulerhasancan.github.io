Privacy Policy for Football Club Empire

Developed by Hasan Can Guler · Last updated 13 October 2026

Gameplay data

The game stores your progress locally on your device, including saved careers and settings. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To serve ads, Google collects and uses your device's advertising ID. This may be used to show ads relevant to you, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to measure ad performance.

Google's handling of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information ourselves. The app offers optional in-app purchases processed by Apple’s App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.
